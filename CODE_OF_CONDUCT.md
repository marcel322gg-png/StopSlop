# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

StopSlop contributors and participants are expected to communicate respectfully and constructively. The project welcomes good-faith technical criticism, bug reports, and differing opinions.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and specific.
- Critique code and ideas, not people.
- Share reproducible evidence where possible.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish credentials, personal data, private logs, or another person's identifying information.
- Respect project licensing and upstream attribution.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, deliberate disclosure of private information, spam, and repeated disruptive conduct are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

The repository maintainer may edit, reject, or remove contributions and restrict participation when necessary to keep the project constructive and safe. For conduct concerns, contact the repository owner through a private contact method listed on their GitHub profile. Do not use public issue comments to expose private disputes or personal information.
